I am based in Cambridge at [Microsoft Research Health Futures](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-health-futures/). My work focuses on representation learning for computational pathology, model interpretability, and AI-driven biomarker discovery.

Previously, I completed a PhD in Computer Science at [Mines Paris – PSL](https://www.minesparis.psl.eu/), supervised by [Thomas Walter](https://thomaswalter.github.io/) and [Etienne Decencière](https://people.cmm.minesparis.psl.eu/users/decenciere/) at the [CBIO lab](https://cbio.mines-paristech.fr/).
