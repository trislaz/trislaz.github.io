I am a researcher in computational pathology, but am also broadly interested in mathematics, biology and evolution.
I recently graduated with a PhD in Computer Science from [Mines ParisTech](https://www.minesparis.psl.eu/) and [PSL University](https://psl.eu/),
a work I carried out under the supervision of [Pr. Thomas Walter](https://thomaswalter.github.io/) and [Pr. Etienne Decencière](https://people.cmm.minesparis.psl.eu/users/decenciere/) at the [CBIO lab'](https://cbio.mines-paristech.fr/).
My research focuses on representation learning in histopathology and its application in biomarker prediction.
I am enthusiastic about collaborations and exploring new opportunities. Feel free to reach out to me!
