:page_facing_up: Our paper on predicting intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma transcriptomic classes from routine histology using self-supervised learning is published in [JHEP Reports](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhepr.2025.101675)! Beyond validating SSL-based molecular subtyping on both biopsies and surgical specimens, we uncover an intriguing influence of training-set constitution: good molecular alignment appears more beneficial than raw data quantity for learning meaningful representations.
